YOUR ADVENTURE GUIDE

How to play Word Quest

01Choose your levelPick the age group that suits you: Little Lights, Faith Explorers or Word Masters.
02Beat the clockYou get 60 seconds per question. Correct answers earn points, and faster answers can earn more.
03Build your streakCorrect answers in a row add streak bonuses. One 50:50 lifeline can remove two wrong options.
04Reveal, learn, then nextAfter you answer—or the timer runs out—you'll see the mark, explanation and Bible reference before the next question.
05Grow your rankFinish 50 questions to see your marks out of 50, rank, badges and book-by-book strengths.

Your name, scores and progress stay in this browser using local storage. Clearing browser data can erase them.
